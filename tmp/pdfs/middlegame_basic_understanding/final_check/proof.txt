中文译文

BU-1
One can, without exaggeration, say that the formation and
carrying out of a plan of action is the main task in the course of
the process of the chess struggle.

BU-1

BU-2
A skilfully conceived and purposefully executed plan serves as a
true token of success.

BU-2

BU-3
A skilfully conceived plan - first and foremost this means a
correctly mapped out objective which the chessplayer must direct
his thoughts towards achieving. This problem is far from simple,
and many difficulties arise on the way to solving it. However, a
correctly mapped out objective is only half the matter. It is not
enough to contemplate a plan, it is also necessary to solve
another, no less serious problem - to carry it out. Of course, it is
necessary to look at ways of achieving the objective with an
outline of the objective itself. And what is more, the choice of the
objective to some extent must be determined by the possibility of
achieving it. This possibility, however, has to be assessed in
highly relative terms, since one chessplayer's striving for an
objective will run across the will and intentions of the opponent.
It might also happen that the counter-action of the opponent
forces a change of plan and even a transfer to defence. And yet,
despite the above-mentioned relativity, it is not possible to
conduct a game without drawing up a plan and without directing
one's operations in accordance with it.

BU-3

BU-4
In order to give very simple examples of how to formulate an
idea of a plan, we look at two elementary positions from the
endgame.

BU-4

毫不夸张地说，在国际象棋斗争过程中，制定并执行行动计划
是主要任务。

构思精巧并有目的地贯彻执行的计划，是成功的可靠保证。

所谓构思精巧的计划，首先是指正确确定一个目标，棋手必须
把自己的思考引向如何实现这一目标。要解决这个问题远非易
事，途中会遇到许多困难。然而，正确确定目标只完成了事情
的一半。仅仅设想一个计划并不够，还必须解决另一个同样重
大的问题：把计划执行出来。当然，在勾勒目标本身的同时，
就要考虑实现目标的途径。不仅如此，目标的选择在一定程度
上还必须取决于实现它的可能性。不过，这种可能性必须从很
强的相对性来衡量，因为一方棋手对某个目标的追求，会受到
对手意志和意图的阻碍。对手的反击也可能迫使棋手改变计划
，甚至转入防御。尽管存在上述相对性，没有计划，也不按计
划指导自己的行动，就不可能进行一盘棋。

为了用非常简单的例子说明如何形成计划构想，我们来看两个
基础残局局面。

In positions where there are considerable forces operating (a
characteristic of the central stage), the essence of the plan
remains the same, but, of course, on the whole the game is
complicated immeasurably. The defending side will have
available far more possibilities of resistance, indeed also the
attacking side has in prospect to make a choice between many
objectives and ways to achieve them. The ability to choose the
best and most correct from these is usually determined by the
correctness of the evaluation of the position.

BU-12
Many inexperienced amateurs assume that in the starting position
it is not possible to draw up a relatively real plan of action, since
this is very complicated in that on each of White's moves, Black
has the possibility of replying in various ways. To some extent
this is of course true. And yet, nowadays, in a period of stormy
growth of creative ideas in chess art, and hence a continuous
enrichment of its theory, we know only too well that the opening
position is unclear. Already after the first, second and third
moves, variations are obtained out of which arise objectives, not
only in the sphere of purely the opening, but also in the whole of
the future struggle. In selecting his first move, be it 1 c4, 1 d4, 1
Nf3 or 1 e4, the modern chessplayer is already contemplating
several positions which he wants to achieve in the opening and
which, in their turn, will be for him the initial point for working
out a general strategical plan, which he will realise mainly in the
middlegame.

BU-12

BU-13
The planned process of the chess struggle can be subdivided into
three stages:

BU-13

- the preparation for the general battle;
- the struggle for the advantage, and
- the realisation of the advantage.
The success of the first stage, to a considerable extent, is decided
in the opening. The main and most complicated stage is
undoubtedly the second. The third stage is getting results.

许多经验不足的业余棋手认为，从初始局面不可能制定出比较
切实可行的行动计划，因为白方每走一步，黑方都有多种应法
，问题过于复杂。在一定程度上，这当然是事实。然而，如今
国际象棋艺术中的创造性思想正迅速发展，其理论也随之不断
丰富；我们十分清楚，初始局面的发展方向并不明朗。仅仅走
过第一步、第二步和第三步，各种变化就会出现，并由此产生
相应的目标；这些目标不仅属于纯粹的开局范畴，还会影响未
来整个斗争过程。现代棋手在选择第一步时，无论是1 c4、1
d4、1 Nf3还是1 e4，都已经在设想若干自己希望在开局中达到
的局面。这些局面又将成为他制定总体战略计划的出发点，而
这一计划主要在中局实现。

有计划的棋局斗争过程可以分为三个阶段：
- 为全面战斗做准备；
- 争取优势；
- 兑现优势。
第一阶段能否成功，在很大程度上取决于开局。主要而且最复
杂的无疑是第二阶段。第三阶段则是取得成果。
