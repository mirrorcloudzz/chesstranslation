# Instead of an Epilogue

## EP-1

In the chess struggle, enterprise and foresight, boldness and composure, daring and persistence, ingenuity in imagination and accuracy of calculation, allow the scientific process of the chess game to become creative. In other words, with the production of creative forms, chess can be placed alongside works of art.

## EP-2

The present book calls upon chessplayers to strive for creativity. In addition, it calls for a deep respect and study of chess theory and endeavours to do everything in its power to help chess enthusiasts achieve these important objectives.
