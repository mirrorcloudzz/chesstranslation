## PF-2 | 5 | paragraph
EN: Attacking the enemy king is one of the most exciting parts of chess, but it is also one of the hardest to play accurately. Every chess player has had the experience of seeing a promising-looking attack crumble into dust, whereupon the enemy counter-attack sweeps aside everything in its path. Vuković's excellent book The Art of Attack in Chess is a thought-provoking attempt to explain why some attacks succeed while other fail to reach their goal. The author himself points out that not everything in chess can be reduced to a set of rules, but the general principles he establishes in this book provide excellent guidance on when to launch an attack and how to ensure that it has the maximum chance of success.
ZH: 攻击对方的王，是国际象棋中最令人兴奋的内容之一，也是最难走得准确的内容之一。每位棋手都经历过这样的情形：原本看似大有希望的进攻，转眼间土崩瓦解，对手随即发动反击，势不可挡。武科维奇的佳作《国际象棋进攻的艺术》试图解释，为什么有些进攻能够成功，有些却无法达到目的；他的探讨发人深省。作者本人也指出，国际象棋并非所有事情都能归纳为一套规则，但他在本书中提出的一般原则，对何时发动进攻、怎样让进攻尽可能取得成功，提供了极有价值的指导。

## PF-3 | 5 | paragraph
EN: As usual with classic books I have edited, I will take a little time to explain exactly how I have prepared this new edition. First and foremost is the conversion to algebraic notation. In addition to this, I have added 128 extra diagrams and in some cases, where it seemed appropriate, I have added further moves from the quoted games (for example, by giving the actual finish instead of 'and White won in another ten moves'). I have lightly edited the rather ponderous English of the original translation, and brought the method of displaying variations into line with current practice.
ZH: 与我编订其他经典著作时一样，这里也略作说明，介绍新版具体做了哪些工作。首先，也是最重要的一项，是将棋谱改为代数记谱。此外，我增添了128幅棋图，并在适当的地方补入了所引对局的后续走法，例如，不再只写“白方又走了十步便获胜”，而是列出实战的收尾过程。我还对原英译本较为冗赘的文字作了小幅润色，并按照现行惯例调整了分析变化的呈现方式。

## PF-4 | 5 | paragraph
EN: The index of the original book included only complete games, but I have also indexed the game excerpts. In two of the complete games, Alekhine-Asztalos and Alekhine-Kmoch, I have brought the moves of the game into line with the move-order given in the original tournament books.
ZH: 原书索引只收录完整对局，我则把对局片段也列入了索引。在阿廖欣对阿斯塔洛什、阿廖欣对克莫赫这两盘完整例局中，我还依据原始比赛专著所载的次序，调整了实战走法的顺序。

## PF-5 | 5 | paragraph
EN: There are also a fair number of analytical footnotes (readers should note that where there is no room for a footnote on a particular page, the footnote appears on the following page). I hope readers will not form the opinion that Vuković's analysis was especially unsound — this is certainly not the case. The fact is that most authors are prepared to quote the annotations of famous players uncritically, but Vuković brought his own talents to bear and subjected all the positions to careful analysis.
ZH: 本版还加入了相当数量的分析脚注。请读者注意：某页若容不下脚注，它就会排在下一页。我希望读者不要因此认为武科维奇的分析特别不可靠——事实绝非如此。实际情况是，多数作者乐于不加审视地引用著名棋手的评注，而武科维奇发挥自己的才智，对书中每个局面都作了认真分析。

## PF-6 | 5 | paragraph
EN: Quite often the results were startling; in the well-known game Alekhine-Botvinnik, Nottingham 1936 he found a flaw which had eluded the many annotators who simply accepted the version of events Alekhine gave in the tournament book. Inevitably, the high percentage of original analysis implies a greater risk of the occasional slip, but personally I far prefer original comment to a bland recital of the 'party line'. In any case, it is Vuković's general principles which will prove of most value to the practical player, and here there can be no argument about the enduring quality of his work.
ZH: 分析的结果往往令人惊讶。例如，在1936年诺丁汉的阿廖欣对鲍特维尼克这盘名局中，他找出了一处漏洞；许多评注者只是接受阿廖欣在比赛专著中的说法，因而都没有发现它。独立分析所占的比重越大，偶尔出错的风险自然也越高。但就我个人而言，与平淡地复述众口一词的说法相比，我更欣赏独立的评注。无论如何，对实战棋手最有价值的，还是武科维奇提出的一般原则；在这一点上，他著作的持久价值是毋庸置疑的。
