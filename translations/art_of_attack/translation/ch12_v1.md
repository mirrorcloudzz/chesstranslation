## CH12-3 | 334 | paragraph
EN: The attentive reader who has battled his way with the writer from chapter to chapter will have observed the steady widening of the problem of the attack on the king; we began with the basic techniques of combination and have progressed to the kind of understanding which characterizes mastery of the game. ## CH12-4 | 334 | paragraph
EN: From our examination of the defence against a kingside attack and of the phases of such an attack, it has become clear that a correct attack is conditioned by the situation on all parts of the board and also by all that has happened in the earlier course of the game. An attack on the king is not some strange, exceptional, and daring operation like a grand slam in bridge. It is an action which is both logical and necessary; an action on which a player decides at a certain stage of the game; sometimes, indeed, he must decide on it, if his opponent is not to obtain an advantage or if the position is not to fizzle out into a draw.
ZH: 通过考察抵御王翼进攻的方法及进攻的各个阶段，我们已经明白：正确的进攻取决于棋盘各处的形势，也取决于此前整盘棋的发展。杀王进攻并不是桥牌大满贯那样奇异、特殊而大胆的行动，而是合乎逻辑、确有必要的行动，是棋手在对局某一阶段作出的决定。有时，为了不让对手取得优势，或不让局面逐渐消解为和棋，他甚至必须作出这样的决定。

## CH12-5 | 334 | paragraph
EN: Lasker observed long ago that every action is essentially a diversion, a transition from equality to some spatial or temporal commitment. An attack on the king demands a very large diversion both in terms of the number of pieces involved and of the degree of commitment arising out of any pawn moves it entails. A considerable diversion of this kind also logically needs a considerable number of preconditions, as well as a knowledge of how these conditions are interrelated.
ZH: 拉斯克早就指出，任何行动本质上都意味着力量的转移，也意味着从均衡状态转向在空间或时间上作出某种难以收回的投入。杀王进攻所需的力量转移很大：参与的棋子很多，随之而来的冲兵又会使局面发生程度不等的不可逆变化。如此大规模的力量转移，合乎逻辑地要求相当多的先决条件，同时还要求理解这些条件之间的关系。

## CH12-6 | 334-335 | paragraph
EN: It follows, therefore, that the attack on the castled king is an extremely difficult operation, which is based on a perfect mastery of all the forms of chess technique. Even in the early stages of the opening the first seeds of the preconditions for an attack on the king are already being sown, and the tense struggle for the central squares is tightly bound up with the successful outcome of the attack or defence. The characteristic features of a position, the weaknesses on both sides, and the elements that affect the endgame — all these accumulate and become intertwined in such a way that at a certain moment they impel the player to decide on the final objective of his play.
ZH: 因此，攻击易位王是一项极其困难的行动，需要全面、熟练地掌握各种棋艺技术。早在开局初期，杀王进攻先决条件的种子就已播下；对中心格的紧张争夺，与此后进攻或防守的成败密切相连。局面的特点、双方的弱点，以及影响残局的各种因素，不断积累、交织，到了某一时刻，就会促使棋手决定自己行棋的最终目标。

## CH12-7 | 335 | paragraph
EN: This is the moment of crisis in the drama of the games of great masters, when it has to be decided whether it is best to continue promoting the preconditions for an attack on the king or to seek a favourable ending. The decision is not easy, for both courses demand not only technique but the ability to weld all the elements involved into a whole; moreover, we are still in the dark about many of the laws governing this process.
ZH: 这就是大师对局这出戏剧中的关键时刻：应当继续创造杀王条件，还是谋求有利残局？这一决定并不容易，因为两条道路都不仅要求技术，还要求把所有相关因素融合成一个整体；而支配这一过程的许多规律，我们仍然没有弄清。

## CH12-8 | 335 | paragraph
EN: Chess skill at the present time comprises a mastery of combinative techniques and the possession of mature positional judgment, while the science of the game has cleared up the problem of the centre in the majority of openings. However, we are still a long, long way from a thorough knowledge of the laws governing the methods of integrating all the elements into a strategic whole. And where there is insufficient knowledge one proceeds 'by feel', or in other words, one takes risks. The endgame and the attack on the king are two areas about which our knowledge is incomplete, but they are also two directions in which chess can be further explored and through which the ancient game can be raised to a still higher level.
ZH: 就本书写作时而言，高超棋艺包括熟练的组合技术和成熟的局面棋判断；国际象棋理论也已阐明了大多数开局中的中心问题。然而，要彻底认识如何把各种因素整合为战略整体，我们还相去甚远。知识不足时，就只能“凭感觉”行动，换言之，就是冒险。残局和杀王进攻，是我们认识尚不充分的两个领域；同时，它们也是继续探索国际象棋的两个方向，能够把这项古老的游戏推向更高的水平。

## CH12-10 | 335 | heading
EN: The discoveries of Alekhine and Capablanca
ZH: 阿廖欣与卡帕布兰卡的发现

## CH12-11 | 335-336 | paragraph
EN: It is a slightly strange thesis that one should go back to the period before the Second World War and look for the culmination of the art of the attack on the king in the play of Alekhine and Capablanca. For have not Botvinnik and Smyslov, Keres and Bronstein, Reshevsky and Gligorić in their rich creativity in the field of chess discovered all that Alekhine and Capablanca knew and also gone on to add something else? It is indeed true that they have proceeded further in many respects, and perhaps even in their knowledge about the attack on the king they are the equals of the great masters of the past; in their actual games, however, they have not so far put it into practice to the full.
ZH: 主张回到第二次世界大战之前，在阿廖欣和卡帕布兰卡的对局中寻找杀王艺术的顶峰，似乎有些奇怪。难道鲍特维尼克和斯梅斯洛夫、凯列斯和布龙斯坦、雷舍夫斯基和格里戈里奇，在丰富的国际象棋创作中，不仅已经掌握了两位前辈的全部知识，而且还增添了新的内容吗？确实，他们在许多方面都走得更远；也许就杀王进攻的知识而言，他们也不逊于过去的大师。然而，在实际对局中，他们迄今尚未把这种认识充分付诸实践。

## CH12-12 | 335-336 | paragraph
EN: If we delve into the whole of chess literature looking for games which serve to illuminate the logical integration of the attack on the castled king and the game in its entirety, then those of Alekhine and Capablanca remain the unsurpassed models; side by side with them may be placed a few of the games of Rubinstein, Keres, and Bronstein, though we shall find scarcely any new discoveries in them: the whole period from Alekhine's death to the present day has not been particularly fruitful in the sphere of the attack on the king. So one is justified in going back to Alekhine and Capablanca as the first to discover knowledge which to this day has been neither refuted nor superseded.
ZH: 如果遍览国际象棋文献，寻找能够说明怎样把攻击易位王合乎逻辑地融入整盘棋的对局，阿廖欣和卡帕布兰卡的作品仍是未被超越的典范。鲁宾斯坦、凯列斯和布龙斯坦的少数对局，可以与之并列，但其中几乎没有新的发现。从阿廖欣去世到本书写作时，这整个时期在杀王进攻领域并没有取得特别丰硕的成果。因此，回到阿廖欣和卡帕布兰卡，是有道理的：他们最先发现的那些知识，直到本书写作时仍未被推翻，也未被取代。

## CH12-13 | 336 | paragraph
EN: As far as these actual discoveries by Alekhine and Capablanca are concerned, it can be said that they did not take the form of some new principle but rather depended on a new way of thinking, which proceeded subjectively and therefore in a manner which cannot be expressed or comprehended on a theoretical basis; by these methods they discovered moves which fulfil the chief principle of the attack on the castled king, namely, obtaining the maximum preconditions for an attack with the minimum of commitment.
ZH: 至于阿廖欣和卡帕布兰卡究竟发现了什么，可以说，那并不是某条新原则，而是一种新的思维方式。这种思考带有个人特性，因而不能完全用理论表述和理解。他们凭借这种方法发现的走法，体现了攻击易位王的首要原则：以最小限度的投入，创造尽可能充分的进攻条件。

## CH12-14 | 336 | paragraph
EN: This principle in itself is a simple one and can be easily remembered, but playing in accordance with it is extremely difficult. Here two grades of values must be clearly distinguished: the one based on the value of the individual preconditions and the other on that of the successive commitments undertaken. This must then be resolved into a precisely ordered series of moves, while one must also be ready at any moment to convert the attack into some other thematic course of action — all of which requires considerable mastery.
ZH: 这条原则本身很简单，也容易记住，但要照着它下棋，却极其困难。这里必须分清两套价值尺度：一套衡量各项先决条件的价值，另一套衡量接连作出的投入。随后，还必须把这些判断落实为次序准确的一连串走法，同时随时准备把进攻转化为其他符合局面主题的行动。这一切都需要很高的棋艺。

## CH12-15 | 336 | paragraph
EN: From a close study of the games of Alekhine and Capablanca one can conclude that they did indeed use a perfected method of thinking in the sense mentioned above; only, in the case of Alekhine this thinking was more analytical and logical, while with Capablanca it was more artistically intuitive. However, the results of this thinking, that is, the actual moves, do not appear to differ greatly to the outside observer.
ZH: 仔细研究两人的对局，可以看出，他们确实运用了上述意义上的完善思维方法。只是阿廖欣的思考更富于分析性和逻辑性，卡帕布兰卡则更多依靠艺术家的直觉。不过，旁观者从思考的结果，也就是具体走法来看，两者似乎并没有很大差别。

## CH12-16 | 336 | paragraph
EN: However, Alekhine's discoveries can be somewhat more precisely defined than Capablanca's: Alekhine's output was more abundant, he 'lived chess' more deeply, and he was a more passionate analyser of his own games. A particular characteristic of his commentaries is the frequent use of the idea of commitment (just as with Lasker we get the idea of compensation, with Steinitz that of the balance of the position, and with Nimzowitsch that of centralization). One could say that of all masters Alekhine had the most refined sense of the degree of commitment, even though his temperament sometimes led him to be too eager.
ZH: 不过，相较于卡帕布兰卡，阿廖欣的发现可以界定得更准确一些：他的作品更多，对国际象棋的投入更深，对分析自己的对局也更有热情。他的评注有一个显著特点，就是经常运用“投入”这一观念，正如拉斯克强调补偿、斯坦尼茨强调局面的均衡、尼姆佐维奇强调中心化一样。可以说，在所有大师中，阿廖欣对这种投入程度的感觉最为细腻，尽管他的性格有时仍会使他操之过急。
