# 导言

## IN-1 | 6 | title
EN: Introduction
ZH: 导言

## IN-2 | 6 | paragraph
EN: Action is the essential basis on which the game of chess is founded and any action which contains a threat — i.e. attack in its widest sense — stands out as a prominent feature of the game. To the outside observer, a chess game is dominated by the conflict between the two players. The ultimate aim of each player is, as a rule, the mating of the opponent's king, and an action with this aim, whether it is direct or indirect, is called an attack (in a narrower sense of the word) or a mating attack; that is the subject of this book.
ZH: 国际象棋以行动为根本，而凡是制造威胁的行动——也就是最广义的进攻——都是对局中的突出特征。在旁观者眼中，一盘棋的主线就是双方的交锋。通常，每位棋手的最终目标都是将杀对方的王；以此为目的的行动，无论直接还是间接，都称为狭义的进攻，或杀王进攻。这正是本书的主题。

## IN-3 | 6 | paragraph
EN: Being the most important action in chess and the central element of the game in ancient times, attack appears at all stages of the game's development and in various forms of perfection. Moreover, it can be said that the reforms which have been made in the rules of chess have always been in the direction of increasing and stimulating the opportunities for attack.
ZH: 进攻是国际象棋中最重要的行动，在古代更是对局的核心。因此，在国际象棋发展的各个阶段，我们都能看到进攻以不同形式出现，并逐渐趋于完善。甚至可以说，国际象棋规则历次改革的方向，始终都是增加进攻机会，鼓励进攻。

## IN-4 | 6 | paragraph
EN: The great reform of c. 1485, which created modern European chess, was particularly responsible for opening up new opportunities for attack and ushered in a period of rich development in chess technique. For three centuries of chess history, attack predominated over defence in the practice of the great players, and mastery at that time meant skill in conducting an attack. Only with Philidor did the first positional ideas appear, and with them more mature defensive strategies; these were to find in Steinitz a century later a legislator of genius.
ZH: 约1485年的重大改革奠定了现代欧洲国际象棋的形态，尤其为进攻开辟了新的可能，也由此开启了棋艺蓬勃发展的时期。在此后三个世纪里，伟大棋手的实战始终是进攻压倒防守；当时所谓高超的棋艺，主要就是善于组织进攻。直到菲利多的时代，最初的局面思想才开始出现，更成熟的防守策略也随之诞生。一个世纪之后，斯坦尼茨以天才般的洞察力，为这些思想确立了系统的原则。

## IN-5 | 6-7 | paragraph
EN: During the classic era of chess from Morphy to Steinitz and on to Lasker the value placed on attack gradually decreased, for with greater positional understanding the foundations were also provided for the perfection of defensive technique. This, however, was followed by a new period in which Capablanca, and particularly Alekhine, perfected the technique of attack, above all that of the attack on the castled position, founded on exact positional play. With Alekhine the aggressive and dynamic style of play reached a zenith; in the period which followed the tide again turned away gradually from the risks of the direct attack in search of new paths.
ZH: 在从墨菲到斯坦尼茨，再到拉斯克的古典时期，人们对进攻的重视逐渐下降，因为对局面认识的加深，也为防守技术的完善奠定了基础。然而，随后又出现了一个新的时期：卡帕布兰卡，尤其是阿廖欣，以准确的局面走法为基础，完善了进攻技术，特别是攻击易位局面的技术。积极、富于动态变化的棋风，在阿廖欣手中达到了顶峰。此后，潮流又逐渐避开直接进攻所带来的风险，转而寻找新的道路。

## IN-6 | 6-7 | paragraph
EN: The main reason for this is not to be found in any weakness inherent in the attacking style, but in the simple fact that, given the conditions of present-day tournaments, it is more profitable and advantageous to make a study of openings. Now, it must be understood that the present theory of openings represents a detailed development of Nimzowitsch's ideas concerning the central squares. There are still many gaps to be filled and there is a wealth of opportunity for innovation; as a result, great masters are inclined to concern themselves intensively with openings and to opt for a 'safety-first' style.
ZH: 主要原因并不在于进攻型棋风本身有什么缺陷，而在于一个简单的事实：在当时的比赛条件下，深入研究开局更容易取得成效，也更有利。应当理解，当时的开局理论，是对尼姆佐维奇有关中心格思想的细致发展。其中仍有许多空白，也存在大量创新机会。因此，顶尖棋手往往专注于开局研究，并选择“安全第一”的棋风。

## IN-7 | 7 | paragraph
EN: When this source of opening innovations begins to dry up, the problem of attack will present itself once more. The time may even come when the principles on which Alekhine built up his attacks will be completely understood, and those ideas which in the case of Alekhine had the appearance of a spark of genius will take on the more approachable aspect of attacking technique. It will be more convenient to discuss these questions at the end of the book.
ZH: 当这一开局创新的源泉开始枯竭时，进攻问题就会再次摆到人们面前。也许有一天，人们会彻底理解阿廖欣构筑进攻所依据的原则；那些在阿廖欣手中仿佛天才灵光一现的构思，也会成为更容易掌握的进攻技术。这些问题留到本书结尾讨论更为合适。

## IN-8 | 7 | paragraph
EN: At this point it should be enough to point out that there exists an extremely large group of chess players, who are no longer beginners nor, on the other hand, masters or point-hunters, but players who aim primarily at deriving an aesthetic satisfaction from the game. For such players an attacking game is more attractive than positional technique and they will continue to attack regardless of risk, for their stormy contests are not going to be noted down in theoretical textbooks. So why should such players not become acquainted with the general principles of attack and why should they not perfect themselves in that style of chess with which they are most at home?
ZH: 这里只需指出，棋手中有一个极为庞大的群体：他们已经不是初学者，却也不是大师，更不是一心追逐积分的人；他们下棋，主要是为了从中获得审美的满足。对这些棋手来说，进攻型对局比局面技术更有吸引力。他们仍会不顾风险地进攻，因为他们那些激烈的交锋，本就不打算写进理论教科书。那么，他们为什么不该了解进攻的一般原则，又为什么不该在自己最擅长的棋风中不断提高呢？

## IN-18 | 9 | heading
EN: The basic pattern of mate
ZH: 将杀的基本模式

## IN-19 | 9 | paragraph
EN: For mate to be obtained, the king must be deprived of nine squares if it is in the middle of the board, six squares if it is on the edge, or four if it is in the corner. Some of these squares may be blocked by the king's own supporting pieces, but the rest must be taken by the attacker through the agency of his own pieces or pawns. If all the squares surrounding the king are taken from it — some through being blocked, others by being 'in check' — and if the square on which the king stands is also in check and without means of defence, then the king is checkmated.
ZH: 要完成将杀，若王在棋盘中部，就必须封住包括它所在格在内的九个格；若王在边线上，则是六个格；若在角落，则是四个格。其中有些格可能被王己方的棋子占据，其余格则必须由进攻方的棋子或兵控制。如果王周围的格都已不能进入——有些被堵住，有些受到攻击——同时王所在的格也受到攻击，而且无法化解将军，王就被将杀了。

## IN-22 | 10 | paragraph
EN: The final or mating position is called the mating pattern, and this term is especially useful when such a pattern is worth remembering. A mating pattern can be typical (i.e. one which frequently occurs) or atypical. We call a mate (or mating pattern) pure if none of the squares concerned is covered by two or more attacking units. Thus 1... Ne2 2. Qe6 is a pure mate, whereas 1... Kf6 2. Bg7 is not, though it would be if we were to remove the bishop on f5. Pure mates are valued in chess problems, but in practical play they are unimportant.
ZH: 最终形成的将杀局面，称为“将杀模式”。当这种模式值得记忆时，这个术语尤其有用。将杀模式可以是典型的，也就是经常出现的，也可以是非典型的。如果相关的格中，没有任何一格同时受到两个或更多进攻子力的控制，我们就把这样的将杀或将杀模式称为“纯净将杀”。因此，1... Ne2 2. Qe6是纯净将杀，而1... Kf6 2. Bg7则不是；不过，若把f5象移去，后者也就成了纯净将杀。纯净将杀在排局中受到重视，但在实战中并不重要。

## IN-23 | 10 | paragraph
EN: In the mating patterns which we have seen, some of the black king's flight squares have been blocked by its own pieces, while the rest have been covered by the opponent's forces. The sum of these combined factors is known as a mating net.
ZH: 在刚才的将杀模式中，黑王的部分逃跑格被己方棋子堵住，其余格则由对方子力控制。这些因素共同构成了“杀网”。
