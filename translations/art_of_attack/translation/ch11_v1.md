## CH11-7 | 293 | heading
EN: Types of preconditions
ZH: 先决条件的类型

## CH11-8 | 293 | paragraph
EN: Preconditions are naturally contained in the position itself, whether in the attacker's territory or his opponent's.
ZH: 先决条件自然蕴含在局面本身，既可能在进攻方阵地，也可能在对手阵地。

## CH11-9 | 293 | paragraph
EN: The attacker, for instance, may have his pieces strongly deployed in the vicinity of the enemy king position or aimed in that direction, and it may also be that the general mobility of his pieces or his advantage in space assists him in attacking.
ZH: 例如，进攻方子力可能有力地部署在敌王附近，或指向那里；也可能是子力整体机动性较好，或空间优势有助于进攻。

## CH11-10 | 293 | paragraph
EN: At the same time, there must be some deficiencies in the defender's camp; the chief of these is the vulnerability of the king position, either through weaknesses in the pawn structure or because pieces are misplaced in relation to the castled position, being overburdened by obligations to cover squares on other parts of the board.
ZH: 同时，防守方阵地必须存在某些缺陷，主要是王的位置容易受攻击：或者兵形有弱点，或者棋子对王城而言位置不当，因为它们忙于保护棋盘其他地方的格子，负担过重。

## CH11-11 | 294 | paragraph
EN: These and similar preconditions should generally be fulfilled at the first or initial phase of the attack, but it may also be that they can be created by force at this stage, e.g. by the provocation of weaknesses.
ZH: 这些及类似先决条件，一般应在进攻的第一阶段，也就是起始阶段具备；不过，也可以在这个阶段用强制手段创造，例如诱使对手产生弱点。

## CH11-12 | 294 | paragraph
EN: The second phase of an attack can follow a wide variety of courses. It may be a question of a quick attack leading to mate, usually by means of a sacrifice, and in this case it is naturally unimportant to talk of further preconditions. However, if the attack is slower or does not have an uncompromising character, the second phase is usually concerned with the creation of further conditions as guarantees of success. The following are among the most important operations at this stage: the provocation of further weaknesses; the exclusion of one's opponent's counterattack or its suppression; control of the centre; restraint against a central counter-thrust by one's opponent, and so on.
ZH: 进攻的第二阶段可能以多种方式发展。有时通过弃子迅速将杀，此时自然不必再谈进一步的先决条件。但如果进攻较慢，或并非孤注一掷，第二阶段通常就要继续创造条件，以保证成功。这个阶段最重要的行动包括：诱出更多弱点，排除或压制对手反击，控制中央，遏制对手中央反突破，等等。

## CH11-13 | 294 | paragraph
EN: The third phase of an attack is of course characterized by the execution, either in the form of mate, of obtaining a decisive material advantage, or of being able to call off the actual attack on the king and obtain a positional advantage or favourable ending.
ZH: 进攻的第三阶段，自然以兑现成果为特点：或者将杀，或者获得决定性的子力优势，或者结束直接攻王，转化为局面棋优势或有利残局。

## CH11-14 | 294 | heading
EN: Types of commitment
ZH: 投入的类型

## CH11-15 | 294 | paragraph
EN: To the above-mentioned types of preconditions necessary for an attack there also correspond various types of commitment which the attacker can, and indeed must, enter on, if he is going to attack.
ZH: 与上述进攻必要条件的各种类型相对应，进攻方为了发动攻击，也可以，而且确实必须，承担各种投入及其约束。

## CH11-16 | 294 | paragraph
EN: The commitments which correspond to the first phase are, of course, minor ones, usually a question of tempi. A player, for instance, spends a tempo improving his prospects for an eventual attack, transferring a piece from one side of the board to the other or the like; admittedly, the degree of commitment involved is not great, but it can become significant if the attack on the king is never carried out. It is natural that contemporary masters, in an era of precise opening play, aim first of all at sound moves, which give nothing away to their opponents and constantly strengthen their general positional build-up; the result of play according to this recipe on both sides is that a drawn position is reached. It should also be said that Alekhine [note 1] knew how to instil a little aggressiveness into his moves even in the opening and create some conditions for attack with a minimum of commitments. This is precisely where his skill lay, and it is this which is lacking in many masters.
ZH: 第一阶段相应的投入当然较小，通常只是时间。例如，花一步改善未来的进攻前景，把棋子从一翼调到另一翼，等等。投入程度固然不大，但如果攻王始终未能实现，它也可能变得重要。在开局行棋精确的时代，当代大师自然首先追求稳健着法，不给对手机会，持续加强整体布局；双方都按此行棋，结果就会达到和棋局面。还应指出，阿廖欣【注1】即使在开局，也懂得使着法带上一点攻击性，以最小投入创造一些进攻条件。他的本领恰在于此，而许多大师恰恰欠缺这一点。

## CH11-18 | 295 | paragraph
EN: A somewhat greater degree of commitment is entailed in the second phase. It may be a question here of a sacrifice accompanied by considerable compensation, of the dislocation of a number of pieces, or, in particular, of the advance of the wing pawns, which as a rule represents a fairly far-reaching positional commitment. Still, in this phase too the sensible player aims to conduct his game with a minimum of commitments, unless he is undertaking an uncompromising mating attack that is certain to succeed. It is especially important that the attacker should avoid a pattern of thinking whereby an unclear attack is reckoned as part of his compensation. For instance, he may sacrifice a knight for two pawns and an ‘attack’; when that ‘attack’ peters out, his reckoning naturally falls to the ground.
ZH: 第二阶段需要较大的投入：可能是具有充分补偿的弃子，可能是若干棋子偏离原位，尤其可能是翼兵推进，后者通常意味着影响深远的局面约束。不过，即使在这一阶段，理智的棋手仍会尽量减少投入，除非正在进行必定成功、不容退让的将杀进攻。尤其要避免把尚不明确的攻势算作补偿。例如弃一马换两兵和“进攻”，等这个“进攻”消退，原先的算盘自然就落空了。

## CH11-21 | 295 | paragraph
EN: This position is from the game Botvinnik-Chekhover, Moscow 1935. It is White's move, and he has to decide on how to pursue what is already a promising initiative. He has the upper hand in the centre, a strong knight on e5, and well-placed bishops; all these factors together mean that he has some of the preconditions for an attack on the king, without yet having entered on any commitments. He can therefore be said to be on the threshold of the first phase and must consequently look for further moves which will prepare an attack, while committing himself as little as possible. Thus, it would clearly be out of the question to make a pawn advance with h3 and g4, since Black would then find it easy to parry the main threat, after which White would be left only with pawn weaknesses, to say nothing of the dangers which would arise on the diagonal a8-h1.
ZH: 此局面来自鲍特维尼克—切霍韦尔，莫斯科，1935年。白先，必须决定如何继续发展已有的可观主动权。白方中央占优，e5马强大，双象位置良好；这些因素结合起来，说明已经具备一些攻王条件，却尚未承担任何投入。因此可以说，他刚到第一阶段的门槛，应寻找继续准备进攻、同时尽量少作承诺的着法。显然不能h3、g4推进兵，因为黑方很容易挡住主要威胁，此后白方只剩兵弱点，更不必说a8-h1斜线上将出现的危险。

## CH11-57 | 299 | paragraph
