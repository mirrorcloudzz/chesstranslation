## CH7-1 | 142 | title
EN: 7 Ranks, files and diagonals in the attack on the castled king
ZH: 第七章　攻击易位王时的横线、直线和斜线

## CH7-2 | 142 | paragraph
EN: An action in the vicinity of the castled king can be carried out by any of the pieces, but the long-range ones, the queen, rook, and bishop, are the most effective. They can attack from a distance along the ranks, files, and diagonals. The line concerned has to be 'captured,' i.e. one's own long-range piece has to be placed on it, confrontation by the opponent's corresponding piece has to be overcome, and the line has to be 'cleared' of all enemy influence. Outposts lie on such lines; batteries and pins take place along them. In a mating attack the line may finally witness the execution, i.e. checkmate. In this chapter we shall consider in turn the part played in the attack on the castled king by the ranks, files, and diagonals and reveal some typical operations involving them.
ZH: 任何棋子都可以在易位王附近采取行动，但最有效的是后、车、象这些远程棋子。它们可以沿横线、直线和斜线从远处发起攻击。有关线路必须先被“夺取”：把己方的远程棋子部署其上，战胜对方相应棋子的抵抗，并“清除”敌方对这条线路的一切影响。前哨位于这些线路上；叠子攻击和牵制也沿这些线路展开。在将杀进攻中，这条线路最终还可能成为行刑之地，即完成将杀。本章将依次考察横线、直线和斜线在攻击易位王时的作用，并介绍沿这些线路展开的一些典型行动。

## CH7-50 | 148 | paragraph
EN: In connection with this back-row weakness, something should also be said about the so-called 'escape hole', i.e. the gap made by moving a pawn with the sole purpose of eliminating the danger of mate on the back rank or at least of freeing the rook guarding the rank.
