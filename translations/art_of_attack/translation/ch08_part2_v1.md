## CH8B-23 | 213 | heading
EN: The pawn avalanche
ZH: 兵雪崩

## CH8B-24 | 213 | paragraph
EN: The formation composed of two or three neighbouring pawns on the same rank is often called a ‘phalanx’ in chess literature, but when such a phalanx attacks it can also be suitably described as an ‘avalanche’ or ‘roller’. An attack based on a pawn avalanche generally entails even more positional commitments than the bayonet attack, as it involves advancing two pawns instead of only one. On the other hand, the avalanche's power is also greater than that of the bayonet, for an avalanche drives all the enemy pieces before it and offers more attacking possibilities.
ZH: 两三个相邻兵处于同一横线的阵形，棋书常称为“兵方阵”；这种方阵展开攻击时，也很适合形容为“雪崩”或“滚压”。兵雪崩进攻通常比刺刀进攻承担更多局面上的责任，因为要推进的是两个兵，而非一个。但雪崩的威力也更大，它能把面前的敌子全部赶开，提供更多进攻可能。

## CH8B-25 | 213 | paragraph
EN: When attacking by means of a pawn avalanche, it is usually important that one's opponent should be unable to retaliate in the centre or on the other wing. This is, admittedly, a general condition for any kind of attack on the castled king, but in the case of an attack using pawns it applies even more strictly, since one is more restricted positionally and the actual process of advancing pawns is slower than play with pieces, with the result that one's opponent has greater opportunities for counterplay. The next example will show us a case where the conditions for an attack using the avalanche are favourable.
ZH: 采用兵雪崩时，对手不能在中央或另一翼反击，通常是很重要的条件。诚然，这也是任何攻击已易位王的一般条件；但对兵的进攻，要求更为严格，因为己方在局面上的约束更多，实际推进兵又比用棋子行动慢，对手因而有更多反击机会。下例便是适合采用兵雪崩的情形。
