## CH11B-1 | 310-311 | paragraph
EN: The following three games reflect the differences of two periods and three individual styles in preparing and conducting attacks on the king. The first is a sample of old-time brilliance, showing the unfolding of an overwhelming attack resulting from feeble opening play by the opponent. In the second Black makes a risky experiment, and one of his pawns, wandering lonely among the white pieces on the kingside, remains as a consolation and the germ of the subsequent attack. In the third game the underlying conception of the attack is quite different, its main feature being Capablanca’s rational and cautious method of ensuring that every possible means of counterplay on the part of his opponent is first suppressed.
ZH: 以下三局体现了两个时代、三种个人风格在准备和实施杀王攻势上的差异。第一局展现往日的辉煌：对手开局软弱，导致压倒性攻势展开。第二局黑方冒险试验，一个兵孤独地游荡在王翼白子之间，作为慰藉留了下来，也成为随后攻势的萌芽。第三局的基本构想大不相同，主要特点是卡帕布兰卡理性而谨慎的方法：首先确保对手的一切可能反击都被压制。
