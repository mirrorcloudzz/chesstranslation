# 第一章：攻击尚未易位的王

## CH1-1 | 14 | title
EN: 1 The attack against the uncastled king
ZH: 第一章　攻击尚未易位的王

## CH1-2 | 14 | paragraph
EN: Making a mating attack on a king which had not yet castled was one of the joys of chess players in the past. These days the victim of such an attack is usually someone playing a master in a simultaneous display who has failed to castle at the right time.
ZH: 攻击尚未易位的王，是过去棋手们的一大乐趣。如今，这类进攻的受害者，通常是在车轮战中与大师交手、却未能及时易位的棋手。

## CH1-3 | 14 | paragraph
EN: The initial position of the king before it castles contains two main weaknesses. One is that it is exposed if the e-file is opened up; the second is that the square f7 in Black's position (f2 is the corresponding weak point for White) is vulnerable, since it is covered by the king alone. It is therefore natural that the vast majority of attacks on an uncastled king exploit one of these weaknesses.
ZH: 王尚未易位、仍处于初始位置时，有两个主要弱点。一是e线一旦打开，王就会暴露；二是黑方的f7格——白方对应的弱点是f2——只由王保护，因此容易受到攻击。所以，绝大多数针对未易位王的进攻，都会利用这两个弱点之一。

## CH1-4 | 14 | heading
EN: The attack along the e-file
ZH: 沿e线发动进攻

## CH1-5 | 14 | paragraph
EN: The first and most fundamental condition for an attack along the e-file is that the opponent's king should be on that file, and that for some reason it is impossible or difficult for it to move away. If all the adjacent squares are occupied by the king's own pieces or controlled by the opponent's, its escape is absolutely impossible. However, if the player is simply being prevented from castling, but other squares are not covered, the movement of the king is only relatively restricted; in other words, it can move at the cost of losing the right to castle. Castling can also be thwarted indirectly: for instance, if the king has to guard one of the pieces which is protecting it (e.g. on e7 in Black's case).
ZH: 沿e线进攻的第一个、也是最基本的条件，是对方的王位于这条线上，而且由于某种原因无法或很难离开。如果相邻的格都被王己方的棋子占据，或被对方控制，王就完全无法逃离。不过，如果只是易位受到阻止，其他格却未被控制，那么王的活动仅受到相对限制；换言之，它仍能移动，只是要付出失去易位权的代价。也可以间接阻止易位：例如，王必须保护那个挡在它前面的防守棋子，黑方e7格上的棋子就可能承担这种作用。

## CH1-6 | 14 | paragraph
EN: The second condition for an attack of this kind depends on the attacker's own circumstances. First of all, the e-file should be open, or it should at any rate be in the attacker's power to open it; the attacker should also either have a piece which can control a file (a rook or queen) on the file or be able quickly to post one on it. Besides this, he usually needs to strengthen his pressure on the e-file, for instance by doubling rooks or by attacking one of his opponent's pieces which is on the file and protecting the king.
ZH: 第二个条件取决于进攻方自身的配置。首先，e线应该已经打开，或者至少进攻方有能力将它打开；其次，进攻方应当已经把能够控制直线的重子——车或后——放在e线上，或能迅速调过去。此外，通常还需要加强e线上的压力，例如叠车，或者攻击对方位于e线上、保护王的棋子。

## CH1-7 | 14-15 | paragraph
EN: From these necessary conditions it transpires that in an attack along the e-file there tends to be a chain of defence, and the attack is carried out against the central unit of the chain, that is, the piece protecting the king. If this piece is on the square directly in front of the king (e7 or e2), the attacker may be able to mate by capturing it with his queen (or rook), i.e. by making the square into the focal-point.
ZH: 从这些必要条件可以看出，沿e线进攻时，防守方往往存在一条防守链。进攻的目标就是这条链的关键环节，也就是保护王的棋子。如果这个棋子位于王正前方的格，即e7或e2，进攻方就可能用后或车吃掉它并完成将杀，使这个格成为杀王焦点。
