# 第二章　攻击失去易位权利的王

## CH2-1 | 28 | title
EN: 2 The attack on the king that has lost the right to castle
ZH: 第二章　攻击失去易位权利的王

## CH2-2 | 28 | paragraph
EN: This situation arises when the king is either no longer able to castle or has been driven by the opponent away from the castling area. In spite of these possible differences of origin, the general picture and the problem involved are identical and ought to be dealt with in one and the same chapter.
ZH: 这里所讨论的局面，既可能是王已经无法易位，也可能是王被对手赶离了易位后的藏身之处。虽然成因不同，但局面的基本特征和需要解决的问题是相同的，因此应当放在同一章中讨论。

## CH2-3 | 28 | paragraph
EN: First of all, it must be emphasized that the actual fact that a king has lost the right to castle does not necessarily always justify undertaking an attack aimed at mate (as will be shown by the examples of the voluntary loss of castling rights in chapter three). For an attack of this kind to be feasible, the loss of castling rights must at the same time involve the exposure of the king and an increased vulnerability to attack. In the majority of cases the attack is in fact in order, and we shall only consider such positive cases and not the rest. It only remains to be added that the loss of the right to castle can also entail a further weakness besides the danger which faces the king; that is, communication between the rooks is made more difficult. In fact, an attack may logically direct itself against this lack of co-operation between the rooks, e.g. in a struggle for control of an open file.
ZH: 首先必须强调：王失去易位权利这一事实，本身并不总能成为发动将杀进攻的充分理由。第三章中主动放弃易位权利的例子会说明这一点。要使这种进攻成立，失去易位权利还必须伴随着王的暴露，以及王更容易受到攻击。在大多数情况下，发动进攻确实是合理的；本章只讨论这些可以进攻的局面，不讨论其余情况。另外，失去易位权利除了危及王的安全，还可能带来另一个弱点：两车之间更难取得联系。因此，把进攻的矛头指向两车配合不畅之处，也是顺理成章的，例如通过争夺开放线来利用这一弱点。

## CH2-4 | 28 | paragraph
EN: We shall first examine a few typical examples portraying the drama of the king which has lost the right to castle. Such a drama can be divided up into the following episodes:\n1) spoiling the king's castling chances, or drawing it away from the castling position;\n2) the pursuit of the king across the board by checking; and\n3) the final mating attack in the middle of the board or on the edge.
ZH: 我们先看几个典型例子，看看失去易位权利的王会经历怎样一场戏剧。这场戏可以分为三幕：\n1）破坏王的易位机会，或把王从易位后的藏身之处引出来；\n2）连续将军，在棋盘上追赶王；\n3）在棋盘中央或边缘完成最后的将杀进攻。

## CH2-40 | 33 | paragraph
EN: The pursuit of a king that has lost the right to castle is not, however, always such a successful undertaking as in the examples quoted. There have in fact been many occasions when the hunted king has come out the winner by successfully escaping to safety, and when the whole of his flight turns out in the end to be artificial castling on a large scale!
ZH: 不过，追赶失去易位权利的王，并不总能像上述例子那样成功。实际上，许多被追赶的王最终逃到了安全地带，其一方反而赢得了对局；回头看，王的整段逃亡不过是一次大规模的人工易位！

## CH2-41 | 33 | paragraph
EN: A player who is pursuing a king in such circumstances must therefore keep his eyes open to make sure that the prey does not elude him. Two factors are particularly important: the tempo of the attack and slowly but surely building up a mating net from which the king has no escape. As a matter of fact, this net is often of more importance than tempi, and quiet moves which “spin the threads” often point the correct way, whereas many juicy checks may prove to be mistakes. The position of the king that has lost the right to castle represents, as it were, the attacker's capital, which he has acquired at the price of sacrifices or positional disadvantages. The attacker should retain his hold on this capital without worrying too much about “short-term gains” in the form of tempting checks; the decisive point in the end is whether the king is going to fall or not.
ZH: 因此，在这种情况下追赶王的一方必须保持警觉，防止猎物逃脱。有两个因素尤其重要：进攻中的先手，以及逐步织成一道让王无路可逃的杀网。事实上，杀网往往比先手更重要；那些悄悄“织网”的安静走法，常常才是正确的方向，而许多看似诱人的将军却可能是错误。失去易位权利的王所处的位置，好比进攻方的本钱，是他以弃子或局面棋方面的不利为代价换来的。进攻方应当握住这份本钱，不必过于计较那些诱人的将军所带来的“眼前收益”；最终具有决定意义的，是能否把王将杀。

## CH2-42 | 33-34 | paragraph
EN: These remarks are intended to inspire the reader to be cautious in his pursuit of a king that has lost the right to castle. Two maxims here oppose each other: the first instructs one to keep up the tempo of the attack by means of checks in order to give the king no time to recover its breath; the second warns that it is important to finish off the pursuit by cornering the king and forcing it into a mating position. In practice players struggle to find a balance between these two maxims and on their correct choice success itself depends.
ZH: 这些提醒，是希望读者在追赶失去易位权利的王时保持谨慎。这里有两条彼此牵制的准则：第一条要求用连续将军保持进攻的先手，不给王喘息的时间；第二条提醒我们，必须最终把王逼入绝境，迫使它落入将杀局面，才能结束追击。实战中，棋手需要在这两条准则之间找到平衡；能否作出正确选择，决定着进攻的成败。

## CH2-43 | 34 | paragraph
EN: Now that the problem is clear, it will be examined in a series of practical examples. Smyslov–Florian and Belavenets–Chistiakov are cases where the building of a mating net takes precedence over the pursuit of the king. In Tartakower–Euwe, however, we shall see the opposite, with Euwe hunting his opponent's king without giving it a moment's respite. In the game Chigorin–Caro we have an illustration of when a king's pursuit should be halted and its retreat cut off, or, conversely, what happens if an opportunity to “fix” the king is lost and it reaches a position of safety, as in fact occurred in this game.
ZH: 问题已经明确，下面通过一系列实战例子来考察。斯梅斯洛夫对弗洛里安、别拉韦涅茨对奇斯佳科夫这两局，都表明织杀网比追赶王更重要。塔塔科维尔对尤伟一局则相反：尤伟追赶对手的王，丝毫不给它喘息的机会。齐果林对卡罗一局说明，应当在什么时候停止追赶、转而切断王的退路；反过来，如果错过了把王困住的机会，让它逃到了安全地带，又会怎样——这盘棋实际发生的正是后一种情况。
