The weakness of the second rank  
<!-- AA-p149-l025 -->
The second rank, on which the pawns stand at the start of the game, is generally  
<!-- AA-p149-l026 -->

<!-- AA-p149-l027 -->

<!-- AA-p149-l029 -->

<!-- AA-p149-l030 -->

<!-- AA-p149-l031 -->

<!-- AA-p149-l032 -->
