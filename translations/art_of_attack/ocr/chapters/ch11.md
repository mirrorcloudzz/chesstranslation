<!-- AA-p293-l032 -->

Types of commitment  
<!-- AA-p294-l022 -->
To the above-mentioned types of preconditions necessary for an attack there also  
<!-- AA-p294-l023 -->
correspond various types of commitment which the attacker can, and indeed  
<!-- AA-p294-l024 -->

<!-- AA-p294-l025 -->

<!-- AA-p294-l026 -->

<!-- AA-p294-l028 -->

<!-- AA-p294-l029 -->

<!-- AA-p294-l030 -->

The creation of preconditions  
<!-- AA-p299-l018 -->
The preconditions for a successful attack on the castled king can arise in a num-  
<!-- AA-p299-l019 -->
ber of different ways. Some arise out of the normal process of building up a posi-  
<!-- AA-p299-l020 -->

<!-- AA-p299-l021 -->
such an attack; some occur unnoticed, as it were, in the course of the game; and  
<!-- AA-p299-l022 -->
others are the result of risky or even faulty play by the opponent. Let us leave  
<!-- AA-p299-l023 -->
aside those cases where the preconditions are created 'unnoticed' or through  
<!-- AA-p299-l024 -->
faulty play and turn our attention to those whose origin is conscious and calcu-  
<!-- AA-p299-l025 -->

<!-- AA-p299-l026 -->
ceptions.

<!-- AA-p299-l028 -->

<!-- AA-p299-l029 -->

<!-- AA-p299-l030 -->
position in general.

<!-- AA-p299-l032 -->

<!-- AA-p328-l016 -->
A host of examples could be given to illustrate the transition from an attack on  
<!-- AA-p328-l017 -->
the king into the endgame. It may be a question of heading for a drawn position  
<!-- AA-p328-l018 -->
as a lesser evil to the failure which would result from continuing the attack; it  
<!-- AA-p328-l019 -->
may also be that one variation of the attacker's combination leads to an ending.  
<!-- AA-p328-l020 -->
However, cases such as these do not really belong here. We are only interested at  
<!-- AA-p328-l021 -->
this point in cases where the attacker deliberately calls off the attack because his  
<!-- AA-p328-l022 -->
success is better assured by a favourable endgame.  
<!-- AA-p328-l023 -->
The classic exponent of the liquidation of an attack and the transition into the  
<!-- AA-p328-l024 -->
endgame was Pillsbury, so we shall open a series of examples with one of his  
<!-- AA-p328-l025 -->
games.
